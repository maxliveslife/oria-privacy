Oria Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Oria is a private period tracker. It was built so that your information stays with you and nobody else.

What Oria stores

Oria saves only what you enter: period dates, flow, symptoms, mood, pain level, your name (if you add one), your settings and an optional PIN. All of it is stored on your phone only.

What Oria does not do

Oria has no account, no server and no internet access. It does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. It contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. The developer cannot see your data.

Permissions

Notifications: used only for the reminders you turn on, such as a heads-up before your period. On your lock screen they only say "Oria".
Fingerprint: used only to unlock Oria if you turn it on. Your fingerprint is handled by Android and Oria never sees or stores it.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time from the You tab in the app. Uninstalling Oria also deletes all of its data. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, deleted data cannot be recovered.

Health information

Oria shows general information about periods and pain relief. It is not medical advice. Please talk to a doctor or pharmacist before taking anything.

Children

Oria does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
